# Aurex Bay Tycoon — notes for Claude Code

- Deploying to Vercel + Supabase: follow `DEPLOY-BRIEF.md`.

- No build step. Plain ES modules served statically (Vercel). Keep it that way unless asked.
- Game rules live in `supabase/functions/_shared/engine.js` (time, players, coops, network, actions) and `ops.js` (company production, departments, supplies market); content in `content.js`. The browser demo and the Supabase Edge Function import the same files. Never duplicate rules in the client or in SQL.
- The engine is pure: `apply(city, playerId, action, args, now)` and `advance(city, now)` mutate one plain city object. Anything random uses `Math.random` and runs server-side in online mode.
- Time: 1 game year = `CONFIG.YEAR_MS` (one real week) at speed 1. Time advances in weekly steps (`city.wk`, 48 per year): `weekBoundary` moves stocks, supply prices, releases queued orders (`OPS.weekStep`) and rival auction bids (`LIFE.weekStep`); every 4th week runs `monthBoundary`. Share prices also tick inside the week via `priceAt(city, id, g)` (deterministic walk from the weekly close); always use it instead of `stocks[id].p` for display, value and trades. Income between boundaries is a continuous rate (`rates()`), interpolated by the client.
- Between weekly steps the city runs an agenda (`city.agenda`, `planWeek`/`runAgenda`/`AGENDA` in engine.js): each step plans the coming week at random moments (world card once a quarter, decisions, company happenings from `HAPPENINGS` in content.js, bot moves, film/music hits) and `advance` fires whatever the clock has passed. Queued orders land at their own `at` time (`OPS.releaseQueue`). Influence pays per source on its own anniversary month (`influenceMonth`), not all at New Year.
- The city never ends (no seasons). `talent.js` runs specialists as a market (contracts, stress, boredom, satisfaction, transfers; `p.staff` still lists the roles a player holds so `hasStaff(p, role)` works everywhere, `p.team[role]` is the person). Inactive players (`inactive()`, 4 game months) can lose unprotected companies to hostile bids (`protection()`, `takeoverMonth()` in engine.js).
- After changing the engine run `npm test`.
- Online mode: `supabase/functions/game/index.ts` loads `cities.state`, advances, applies, saves via `save_city` (optimistic concurrency). Browsers only read.
- `ops.js` also holds clients, orders (accept/reject/renegotiate/deliver/archive), the company board and interactive task choices. `logistics.js` holds warehouses, shipments and the three freight carriers (`freight`, `book`, `status`; human purchases/exports go through `ctx.book`) (positions in game years, interpolated on the map). `life.js` holds the personal side (wallet, homes, collections, auction house, trades, prestige); its actions are routed from `apply` by name prefix (life*, home*, item*, bid*, trade*, host). Tests: `tests/engine.test.js`, `tests/orders.test.js`, `tests/life.test.js`.
- UI: `js/main.js` (shell, map, Empire, Market, Network, Coop), `js/company.js` (company dashboard), `js/scenes.js` (20-second interactive tasks: run inline in the box that started them via `SCENES`, keep running across navigation, wait for a decision), `js/interact.js` (delivery map, warehouse modal), `js/life.js` (Life tab, dark warm theme scoped under `.lifep`), `js/web.js` (in-game browser overlay `#web`: fictional city websites for auctions, dealers, homes and news; each site's CSS is scoped under `.s-<site>`), `js/freight.js` (carrier picker, per-company Deliveries box, carrier websites), `js/team.js` (team box, specialist card, Apex Talent site), `js/stocks.js` (exchange), `js/util.js` (formatting, charts).
- Map: two levels (city overview → district). `DISTRICTS` in content.js hold `box` on the city map and an optional own image (`img/dist-<id>.jpg`, `w`, `h`); entities carry `d` + `p` (fractions of the district view) and `placeOn()` derives city x/y. Map UI is in main.js (`setLevel`, `renderMarks`, `mapCardHTML`); image crops via `cropStyle()` in util.js.

- Concrete plant (`concrete` type, L37 Bayside Concrete, Harbor Row) mixes cement, sand, gravel (imported goods) and water (1:2:4:0.5 by weight) plus energy. Water is a mains good (`GOODS.water.mains`): never stocked or ordered, paid as used (`OPS.payMains`; recipes iterate `OPS.stocked(l)` for stock) into concrete, which construction companies use. `RECIPES[type].yard` = most the company holds per good (Warehouse box, `yardBox` in company.js; orders, restocks and warehouse trucks stop at it via `OPS.yardRoom`; raw materials start half full, older saves once via `OPS.fillYard`). `manual: true` = the monthly cycle uses nothing; the owner mixes batches by hand: `mixStart` (`OPS.mixStart`, puts the supplies in the drum, `l.ops.batch`, at most `capacity()` a month and the finished-goods yard), then the slider game in `js/mixer.js` (1 s per tonne, `OPS.MIX`) and `mixEnd` (`ok` puts it in the drum for `MIX.drumT` = 5 s per tonne: `batch.drum`/`batch.ready`, `OPS.mixStep` on every advance moves it to the yard and no new mix starts meanwhile; the browser's `drumTick` in main.js counts down and sends `mixCheck` when it is done; otherwise or after `MIX.grace` the batch is thrown out); bot-run plants still run by the month. Fresh goods (`GOODS[g].fresh`, concrete: 48 real hours) keep lots per batch (`l.ops.lots[g].inv/fg`, `OPS.freshStep` on every advance): what leaves the yard comes off the oldest lot, stock listed on the market still counts, and a lot past its time is thrown out with a notice; the Warehouse box shows each lot's time left. New goods join older cities' markets via `OPS.ensureMarket` (called in `migrateMap`). Tests: `tests/concrete.test.js`.

- Holdings with divisions (co-owned parent companies, `h.div`): `_shared/holding.js` (`HD` from engine.js, numbers in `HOLDING` in content.js). Each human owner whose `p.co.hid` is the holding runs their own empire as a division (companies stay `k:'p'`, `p.cash` = division balance) and is its CEO; the holding's own companies are the joint division run by `h.ceo` (`canRun`). `holdingNav` adds central account (`h.treasury`) + joint division + every division (`HD.empire`), and a member's `netWorth` is stake × NAV + Life. Monthly `HD.monthly`: share of division profit (`h.bidrag`) to the central account, internal loan interest (`h.iloans`), owner loans (`h.oloans`). CEO salary = `p.life.salary`, paid from the central account (`rates('h')`), set by vote (`salary` proposal, cap → unanimous). Actions `holdingTransfer/Borrow/Repay/Deposit/CashOut/Lend/Recall/Give/Grant`, `coMerge` (`holdingGrant` = central account → a division, not a loan: the joint CEO alone up to `HOLDING.grantPct` of NAV, otherwise proposal `grant`); proposal kinds in `HD.KINDS` (dividend, salary, bidrag, ceo, autoEq, equalize, exit, divBuy, divSell, move, grant; `HD.unanimous`). A CEO buys for their division alone (no vote); sells alone up to `HD.mandate`. New Year (`HD.yearEnd`): annual report in `h.reports` (solo tycoons: `p.reports`), mail with `go: 'report:…'`, CEO bonus, annual meeting (`h.ga`, ga proposals default to yes). Leaving = `exit` vote; the last owner keeps everything (`HD.dissolve`). Older parent holdings migrate once (`HD.migrate`). Admin test: game action `adminYearEnd` → `closeBooks`. UI: `js/holding.js` (card on Network, report page `UI.company = 'report:h:<id>[:n|:ytd]'`). Tests: `tests/holding.test.js`.

Rivals (≤ −40, or 3 wins → `rival`) make one move at a time per tycoon (`weekStep`: price war, take an unanswered order, Gazette story, bid for a specialist; enemies ≤ −75 a consent takeover bid `o.consent` that only goes through with the owner's yes and expires otherwise). Friends tip, offer goods under market, stay out of your auctions, warn; allies send the quarter's insight and block other bots' bids (`onHostile`). Stories in `monthStep`: crisis (buy/lowball/lend), dream company bids every half year, alliances of two rivals (`h.vs`), peace after 6 more wins. Bot mails have `from.k = 'bot'` and `opts[].bot` (answered by `BOT.answer`; the autopilot always picks the safe answer). Newcomers are left alone 3 months. After a level start (`equalStart` sets `city.m0`) bots buy companies `CONFIG.BOT_RUSH.x` times as often in months `from`–`to`. UI: bot card in `botProfileHTML` (js/profile.js, initials until portraits exist), mail folder Tycoons, chat with fixed replies `BOT.reply` kept in localStorage (js/chat.js). Tests: `tests/bots.test.js`.

- Private banks: `_shared/banks.js` (`BK` from engine.js, ctx `kx(city)`; numbers in `BANKS` in content.js). Every `bank`/`finance` listing that stands (not L13) lends (`BK.isLender`); owners set the margin over the market rate (`city.bk.cfg[lid]`: `sp`, `msp`, `max`, `hand`, `open`; reset on a new owner), bots use `BANKS.bots[id].spread`, unowned banks the autopilot. Only owners of a bank or of the Bank of Aurex borrow at the market rate (`BK.ownsBank`, loans `{ bank: 'cb', rate: 'bank' }`); everyone else borrows from a bank at a fixed rate (`p.loans[] = { id, amt, rate, m, bank: lid, pledge?, risk? }`), last resort `cb` at market + `lastResort` when no bank stands. All borrowing goes through `BK.borrow` (actions `loan`/`repay`, overdraft cover in `solvency` and bot purchases with `force`). A bank funds what it lends on a funding line at the Bank of Aurex (business loans at the market rate, mortgages at the mortgage rate, floating while the loans are fixed); `BK.funding(city, l, ctx)` gives its account (interest in, funding cost, losses this year from `city.bk.loss`, net, `shock` = net lost per +1 point), shown in the Lending box, the bank office and the governor's Funding lines card. Capital: a bank an active player runs needs equity (`city.bk.cap[lid]`, action `bkCap` in/out) of `BANKS.capital.ratio` (8%) of its risk-weighted loans (mortgages × `mortW` 0.5; `BK.capital`); below it a loan request becomes an application (`offers().croom` < amount → `ask`, mail says how much equity to put in) that the owner approves once there is equity (`room()`), forced borrowing skips it, mortgages need `mroom`; equity funds that part of the business loans (smaller funding line, higher margin), losses come off it first (`hit`), a new owner → the old owner is paid it back in `weekStep`. Bot and unowned banks run without. Private bank sites hide the market rate from everyone but the bank's runners. Margin on business loans is income in `rates()` (`lending`); mortgages (`h.bank`, `h.rate`, `LIFE.homeRate`) come from `BK.mortgage` and pay the bank monthly (`mortPaid`); two months with the wallet in the red → foreclosure auction (`foreclose` in life.js). Credit committee: `hand` banks get applications (`city.bk.apps`, mail with `web` link), owners decide with `bkDecide` (ok/no/rate/pledge counter; counter-offers to players are mails with `opts[].bk` → `BK.answer`), loan officers decide after `wait` weeks by `BK.score`; the 20-second meeting is `openCredit` in js/banks.js. Defaults (`BK.monthStep`/`fail`): players two months in the red, bots by `risk` × economy; the bank takes a pledged company or loses `1 − recovery`; `city.bk.rec` keeps records. Rivals ask human-run banks for loans and start rate wars (`city.bk.war`) in `BK.weekStep`. UI: js/banks.js (each bank's site `<name>.ab`, Harbor Trust at harbortrust.ab/business; Lending box on the dashboard; bank list on bankofaurex.ab/loans). Tests: `tests/banks.test.js`.

Own truck (`LOG.truckMove`, `move: true`, `by` = who sent it), no money, truck days by distance. UI: Move button in Production & supplies, modal `moveHTML` in js/deals.js. Tests: `tests/move.test.js`.
