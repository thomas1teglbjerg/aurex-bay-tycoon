# Aurex Bay Tycoon — notes for Claude Code

- Deploying to Vercel + Supabase: follow `DEPLOY-BRIEF.md`.

- No build step. Plain ES modules served statically (Vercel). Keep it that way unless asked.
- Game rules live in `supabase/functions/_shared/engine.js` (time, players, coops, network, actions) and `ops.js` (company production, departments, supplies market); content in `content.js`. The browser demo and the Supabase Edge Function import the same files. Never duplicate rules in the client or in SQL.
- The engine is pure: `apply(city, playerId, action, args, now)` and `advance(city, now)` mutate one plain city object. Anything random uses `Math.random` and runs server-side in online mode.
- Time: 1 game year = `CONFIG.YEAR_MS` (one real week) at speed 1. Time advances in weekly steps (`city.wk`, 48 per year): `weekBoundary` moves stocks, supply prices, releases queued orders (`OPS.weekStep`) and rival auction bids (`LIFE.weekStep`); every 4th week runs `monthBoundary`. Share prices also tick inside the week via `priceAt(city, id, g)` (deterministic walk from the weekly close); always use it instead of `stocks[id].p` for display, value and trades. Income between boundaries is a continuous rate (`rates()`), interpolated by the client.
- The city never ends (no seasons). `talent.js` runs specialists as a market (contracts, stress, boredom, satisfaction, transfers; `p.staff` still lists the roles a player holds so `hasStaff(p, role)` works everywhere, `p.team[role]` is the person). Inactive players (`inactive()`, 4 game months) can lose unprotected companies to hostile bids (`protection()`, `takeoverMonth()` in engine.js).
- After changing the engine run `npm test`.
- Online mode: `supabase/functions/game/index.ts` loads `cities.state`, advances, applies, saves via `save_city` (optimistic concurrency). Browsers only read.
- `ops.js` also holds clients, orders (accept/reject/renegotiate/deliver/archive), the company board and interactive task choices. `logistics.js` holds warehouses, shipments and the three freight carriers (`freight`, `book`, `status`; human purchases/exports go through `ctx.book`) (positions in game years, interpolated on the map). `life.js` holds the personal side (wallet, homes, collections, auction house, trades, prestige); its actions are routed from `apply` by name prefix (life*, home*, item*, bid*, trade*, host). Tests: `tests/engine.test.js`, `tests/orders.test.js`, `tests/life.test.js`.
- UI: `js/main.js` (shell, map, Empire, Market, Network, Coop), `js/company.js` (company dashboard), `js/scenes.js` (20-second interactive tasks: run inline in the box that started them via `SCENES`, keep running across navigation, wait for a decision), `js/interact.js` (delivery map, warehouse modal), `js/life.js` (Life tab, dark warm theme scoped under `.lifep`), `js/web.js` (in-game browser overlay `#web`: fictional city websites for auctions, dealers, homes and news; each site's CSS is scoped under `.s-<site>`), `js/freight.js` (carrier picker, per-company Deliveries box, carrier websites), `js/team.js` (team box, specialist card, Apex Talent site), `js/stocks.js` (exchange), `js/util.js` (formatting, charts).
- Map: two levels (city overview → district). `DISTRICTS` in content.js hold `box` on the city map and an optional own image (`img/dist-<id>.jpg`, `w`, `h`); entities carry `d` + `p` (fractions of the district view) and `placeOn()` derives city x/y. Map UI is in main.js (`setLevel`, `renderMarks`, `mapCardHTML`); image crops via `cropStyle()` in util.js.
- Company and specialist cards use the dark dossier style (`.sheet.dz`, `companyCard` in main.js, `talentModal` in team.js). `starter: true` on a type or a TALENT entry keeps bots away from it. Listings may carry a `title`; show names with `lname(l)`.
- Company accounts: `OPS.closeMonth(l, m, monthLines(city, l))` writes one hist row per month (b2c, goods, ypi, sup, fr, sal, bud, heads, maint, units, sold, orders); `accountsBox` in company.js turns it into the income statement.
- Department header images: `img/d-<dept>.jpg` are optional; the CSS falls back to the company art.
- Every `.box > h2` in the panel folds its box (`applyCollapsed`/`toggleBox` in main.js, remembered in localStorage per page and title).
- Money below 1 M shows as thousands (`M()` in util.js, and `kfix` inside `esc()` rewrites "0.26 M" in any text). Specialists leading a department add `TAL.headYpi(rating)` × size YPI to that company on top of their role passive.
- UI copy is English; keep it short and concrete.
